# Sagani — Features

> With Sagani, makakasiguro ka sa iyong ani.

---

## 1. Realtime Current Weather Prediction

Current and forecast weather conditions that help farmers plan the day ahead.

| Signal | Description |
|---|---|
| Temperature | Current and expected temperature in the farm's area. |
| Humidity | Current and expected humidity levels. |
| Season | Detected season and its expected effect on the farm. |
| Summary of weather condition | A farmer-friendly summary of the overall weather situation. |
| Future prediction and mitigation | Forecast of upcoming conditions plus recommended preventive actions. |

---

## 2. Farmer Crop Insights

Crop-specific guidance that translates weather and climate data into farm decisions.

| Insight | Description |
|---|---|
| Planting season period | The recommended window for planting based on current and forecast conditions. |
| Fertilization period | When to apply fertilizer for best crop response. |
| Harvesting period | The suggested timing for harvesting. |
| Realtime market price | Current market prices to help with selling decisions. |
| Watering times per day | How many times to water the crop each day based on conditions. |

---

## 3. Water Control / Water Management

Tools that help farmers use available water efficiently.

### Generate a Report

- **Estimated water budget** — how much water is available and how it should be allocated.
- **Suggested recommendations or solutions** — practical actions to improve water use and reduce risk.
